# 21 hook formulas

Formats that short-form creators use again and again, grouped by how they hold the viewer. They're
templates, not guarantees: fill them with something true and specific to you. Examples are in
English with a Lebanese line where it helps; write yours in your own dialect.

## Bold claim (the news)

1. **"[Tool] just killed [thing everyone uses]."** The stake is the thing, not the tool.
   «Claude قتل الإنستغرام.» Line 2 must say plainly what it really does.
2. **"[Big job] done in [small time]."** "11 days of work, done in 29 hours."
3. **"Nobody's talking about [X], and it changes [stake]."**

## Call out the viewer

4. **"If you [situation], don't scroll."** «إذا عندك interview بكرة…»
5. **"If you're still [old way], you're losing [stake]."**
6. **"[Audience], this one's for you."**

## Start at the shocking moment

7. **Open on the result:** "This [result] took me [time]." Show the result in frame 0.
8. **"I [did surprising thing]. Here's what happened."**
9. **Before/after:** "This was [before]. This is [after]." Both on screen by second 2.

## Open question

10. **"Why does [observable thing] [surprising]?"**
11. **"Do you know what [common thing] actually does?"**
12. **"What happens if you [action]?"**

## Broken expectation

13. **"None of this is real."** Then reveal how it was made.
14. **"I didn't make this reel."** True only if a tool really wrote or made it.
15. **"Everyone thinks [belief]. It's the opposite."**

## Promise the method

16. **"How to [result] without [pain]."**
17. **"[N] [things] that [benefit]. The last one is the best."** Then deliver the best last.
18. **"The free way to [expensive result]."**

## Warning

19. **"Stop [common action]."** «وقّف تقدّم على كل وظيفة بتشوفها.»
20. **"The biggest mistake [audience] makes with [topic]."**

## Receipt

21. **"I tested [X] so you don't have to. [Verdict]."** The verdict comes in the hook, not at the end.

## Closers (pair with any hook)

- **Keyword CTA (Instagram):** "Comment [WORD] and I'll DM you [the thing]." Use one uncommon word
  so the auto-DM doesn't fire on everyday comments. Off Instagram, end on a follow line instead.

# reel-week

## Inputs

- `reel-agent/profile.md` (pillars, platforms) and `reel-agent/lessons.md`.
- The latest `reel-agent/research/*.md` from `reel-viral-finder`. If it's older than 7 days, offer
  to run it first.
- The creator's real constraints: how many times they can film this week, and when.
- Their audience's active hours, if they have them (Instagram Insights → Total followers → Most
  active times; TikTok Analytics → Followers). Without them, say the times are a starting guess.

## Plan rules

- **Quality over count.** 3-5 strong posts beat 7 rushed ones. Leave **at least 24 hours** between
  posts on the same account, and don't delete and re-post the same video.
- **Batch the filming.** Group the posts that can be filmed in one session.
- **Mix the pillars,** and mix the hook formula types so the first frames don't all look the same.
- **Every idea has a payoff** the creator can actually show or prove.
- **Reuse what won.** If `lessons.md` names a winner, plan one post that takes its structure further.

## Output: `reel-agent/plan.md`

| Day | Time | Platform(s) | Idea (one line) | Hook formula # | Payoff / proof | Film? | Status |

Status starts as `idea`, then `scripted`, `filmed`, `edited`, `posted`. Below the table, add the
filming session list (what to film together, and what to wear or set up) and the one experiment
this week (what you're testing, and how you'll know).
